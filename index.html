Kids Storytelling

Make Me A Story

A story builder for 5–8 year olds. Parents and kids co-create adventures together using simple building blocks — hero, problem, ending — and let AI weave the magic.

The product question was how to balance child agency with narrative scaffolding. 11 building blocks (hero, sidekick, problem, ending, etc.) give the LLM enough rails to produce coherent narratives without flattening the kid's choices.

Parenting Productivity

School Feed

A single, clean feed for all the emails your kid's school sends. Built with Google Apps Script and shared with other parents on WhatsApp — no more missed notices.

Used by ~25 parents in my local WhatsApp group. Key insight: the value isn't summarisation — it's focus and event detection. Parents need a go-to place to see what's actionable, not sifting through a wall of school emails.

Wellbeing Daily Habit

Daily Pulse

A daily check-in app that logs how you're feeling and surfaces patterns over time. Because understanding yourself is worth a few seconds a day.

5-second check-ins beat 5-minute journals because adherence dominates depth. Not putting any entries is also a signal (mostly "meh" days), and a single day can hold wildly different feelings that one emoji can't capture. Working on making it better.

Parenting Kids

Nature Explorer

A companion for outdoor trips with your kids. Keep them engaged by encouraging them to observe nature as it changes seasons.

The product question was how to make nature observation feel like play, not education. To add natureal variability, I factored in seasons. Season-based prompts give kids a narrowing lens so they're looking for something specific, not just "looking around."
