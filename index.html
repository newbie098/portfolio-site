Kids Storytelling

Make Me A Story

A story builder for 3–8 year olds. Parents and kids co-create adventures together using simple building blocks like hero, problem, superpower, and let AI weave the magic.

The product question was how to balance child agency with narrative scaffolding. 11 building blocks (hero, sidekick, problem, superpower, ending, etc.) with some randomisation baked in, give the LLM enough rails to produce coherent narratives without flattening the kid's choices or becoming repetitive.

Built withPrototyped using Lovable. Moved to Claude Code, OpenAI APIs, Netlify and Supabase to deploy a production-ready version.

Parenting Productivity

School Feed

A single, clean feed for all the emails your kid's school sends. Built with Google Apps Script and shared with other parents on WhatsApp.

Used by ~25 parents in my local WhatsApp group. Key insight: the value isn't summarisation. It's focus and event detection. Parents need a go-to place to see what's actionable, not sifting through a wall of school emails.

Built withGoogle AI Studio & Apps Script, which provided easier integration with Google services.

Wellbeing Daily Habit

Daily Pulse

A daily check-in app that logs how you're feeling and surfaces patterns over time. Because understanding yourself is worth a few seconds a day.

Took a few iterations to get the balance right between gathering meaningful information and keeping it light on inputs. After a couple of months of using it, the 2 insights I gained was that 1) the absense of entries is also a signal (could be "meh" days or very busy days), and 2) a single day can hold wildly different feelings that one emoji can't capture. Working on making it better.

Built withLovable. Multiple iterations to refine the data model and UI/UX.

Parenting Kids

Nature Explorer

An early PoC for a companion for outdoor trips with your kids. Keep them engaged by encouraging them to observe nature as it changes seasons.

The product question was how to make nature observation feel like play, not education. To add natural variability, I factored in seasons and different physical spaces. Both of these work to give kids a narrowing lens so they're looking for something specific, not just "looking around." Needs more validation on the level of questions and tailoring them to specific age groups.

Built withLovable. Multiple iterations to refine the data model and UI/UX.
